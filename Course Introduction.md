CPELX230-330 (Term 2, 2026–2027) explores Agentic Software Engineering and Responsible AI
Examining how autonomous and semi-autonomous AI agents are designed, built, managed, and governed in modern software development.
The course bridges foundational software engineering principles—such as Agile workflows and version control—with intelligent 
Agent architectures and risk management frameworks 
To ensure systems remain reliable, safe, and aligned with human oversight.
